STRATEGY

How MUSCA BTC makes a trade

Protocol --

MUSCA is a two-stage mixture of experts. VWAP is the market-location benchmark; it is not a standalone buy or sell signal.

  1. 01
    Observe the market

    Use completed Binance USD-M candles, trades, order flow, volume, volatility, open interest, basis and funding. Every input must be available before the decision timestamp.

  2. 02
    Measure location around VWAP

    Track distance, velocity, slope, tests and rejections around rolling VWAP across several horizons. This describes where price is and how it arrived there.

  3. 03
    Activate learned experts

    Each frozen expert represents a learned LONG or SHORT market condition and a 1-hour or 6-hour management horizon. An expert activates only when its complete causal rule matches.

  4. 04
    Select the action

    The gating model compares calibrated net EV after Binance costs. It chooses the highest positive action. If no expert has positive EV, FLAT is the neutral and correct decision.

  5. 05
    Execute after the signal

    A paper market order is evaluated on the first valid Binance order-book snapshot after the completed decision candle. The signal candle cannot fill its own order.

  6. 06
    Size by risk

    Planned loss, estimated fees and book impact must fit inside 1% of equity. Margin is capped at 10%, leverage at 10x, and only one position may be open.

  7. 07
    Manage the exit

    Take half at the median favorable excursion, manage the remainder toward the upper target, protect costs, and use a non-widening dynamic trail. The adverse stop always wins when path order is ambiguous.

  8. 08
    Close or time out

    The position closes at target, dynamic stop, trailing stop, invalidation, or the expert's maximum horizon. A new entry is allowed only after the prior position is actually closed.

  9. 09
    Remain fail-closed

    Stale market data, an unsynchronized book, incomplete model inputs or a failed risk check produce WAIT, never a simulated fill.

  10. 10
    Keep paper and real capital separate

    Historical results permit research paper trading only. Real capital remains disabled until the future holdout and all trade-count, stability and execution gates pass.
